Privacy Policy — Senpai Says

Effective: 31 October 2026

Senpai Says lets you hear original anime characters say your name, and set their voice lines as your ringtone, message tone or alarm.

Information handled

The app has no account system and we do not collect, store or share any personal data. The name you type and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising and purchases

This version has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. If that changes in a later version, this policy will be updated first.

Device permissions

Modify system settings is used only when you tap Set to make a voice line your ringtone, message tone or alarm sound. Notifications are used only for the optional daily message, which you turn on in Settings.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.
