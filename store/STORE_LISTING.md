## Full description
Hear an anime girl call **your name** — in real Japanese anime voices or expressive English.

Pick your favourite girl, type your name, and tap a feeling:
💕 Love · 😡 Angry · 🥺 Miss you · 😳 Shy · 🌙 Good night · 📞 Calling

**Meet the girls**
🌸 **Hana** — shy and sweet, she can barely say your name without blushing
🗡️ **Rei** — calm and cool, but she'd protect you from anything
🔥 **Aiko** — tsundere: "It's not like I like you… baka!"
☀️ **Yui** — bubbly and energetic, always cheering you on

**Features**
• Real voice clips in Japanese and English that say your name (1,000+ Japanese names and 430+ English names)
• Choose how they call you: -kun, -chan, -san, -senpai, or just your name
• Special moments: birthday, Eid, exam good luck, gym motivation, "eat your food", take care, thank you
• Set any line as your **phone ringtone**, **message tone** or **alarm sound**
• Share voice clips to WhatsApp, TikTok and Instagram
• Daily message from a different girl every day
• Works offline · no account · smooth on older phones
• Completely free · no ads · no purchases

All characters are original adults. Japanese voices: VOICEVOX:WhiteCUL, VOICEVOX:暁記ミタマ, VOICEVOX:四国めたん, VOICEVOX:猫使アル. English voices: Orpheus TTS.

## Permissions to explain in the review notes
- **Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS):** used only when the user taps "Set" to make a voice line their ringtone, notification sound or alarm sound.
- **Notifications:** optional daily message the user turns on in Settings.
- **Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED):** keeps the optional daily message scheduled after a reboot.
